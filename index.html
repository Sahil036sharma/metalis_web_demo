08 / A FEW GOOD QUESTIONS

Good to
know.

Have another question? Our team is always happy to help.

Talk to a specialist ↗
Can METALIS support a project from design through installation?+

Yes. We can work with you from early concept and technical development through manufacturing, delivery and installation coordination.

Do you offer custom sizes, colours and finishes?+

Our systems are configurable, and our fabrication team can develop bespoke details to suit a project's performance needs and design intent.

What information should I bring to an initial consultation?+

Drawings, schedules, reference images and a sense of your project timeline are useful. If you have them, you can share files in our enquiry form.

Where does METALIS work?+

Our team supports projects across the region and works with design partners on international projects. Get in touch to discuss your location.
